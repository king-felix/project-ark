Quick cards

Everything on this page fits on a few sheets of paper. Print it, fold it, keep a copy in the kitchen drawer, one in the car and one in each bag. Paper works when batteries do not.

1. Water, in four lines

  • Drink first: 3 L per person per day. Never ration drinking water.
  • Boil: rolling boil, 1 minute (3 minutes above 2,000 m).
  • No stove: 2 drops of unscented bleach per litre of clear water, wait 30 minutes.
  • Never drink flood water, sea water, or water that smells of fuel.

2. Bleeding, in four lines

  • Press hard, directly on the wound, with the cleanest cloth you have.
  • Do not lift the cloth — add more on top.
  • Bandage firmly; check fingers/toes stay warm and pink.
  • Lay the person down and keep them warm. Call for help.

3. Someone is not breathing

  • Call for help. Put the phone on speaker.
  • Push hard and fast in the centre of the chest, 100–120 per minute, 5–6 cm deep.
  • Do not stop until help arrives or they breathe.

4. Burns

  • Cool running water for 20 minutes. Not ice, not butter.
  • Remove rings and tight clothing before swelling.
  • Cover loosely with cling film. Do not burst blisters.
  • Bigger than their palm, on face/hands/feet, or white and painless: hospital.

5. Power cut

  • Torch and radio first, candles last.
  • Keep fridge and freezer shut. Fridge food keeps about 4 hours, a full freezer about 48.
  • Unplug computers and TVs against the surge when power returns.
  • No generator, barbecue or stove indoors. Ever.

6. Water leak in the house

  • Close the nearest isolation valve, then the main stop valve.
  • Open the lowest tap to drain the pressure.
  • Bucket, towels, and wrap the pipe with self-amalgamating tape.
  • Move what is below. Dry everything within 24 hours.

  • Open http://ark.lan on any phone or laptop joined to The-Ark-Offline.
  • No password needed to read, watch or download anything.
  • If a "sign in to Wi-Fi" window appears, close it and type the address above.
