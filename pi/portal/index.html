Where your place is kept

Two different promises, and it matters which one you are relying on.

  • A film's resume point Kept in this browser, on this device. It survives closing the tab and restarting the phone. A different phone starts the film from the beginning — the box does not keep a watch history, by design.
  • A game save Kept on the Ark, under a player name you choose. That is the only reason the box asks for a name: without one there is nowhere to put a save, so guest play does not keep one.
  • A map you downloaded Kept in this browser, on this device. Another phone has to download its own copy, and removing it here never touches the Ark or anybody else's phone.
  • A message or a shared file Kept on the Ark, where everyone on this Wi-Fi can see it.
