How Maps works

Back to Maps

Maps is meant to be useful after the Ark is out of reach. That means three separate things have to be true: the region must be downloaded, the page itself must be kept on the device, and the device must trust the Ark’s secure address. This page explains each one in plain words.

1. Download a region

Open Maps while you are connected to the Ark. Choose a region and tap Download for offline use. The page shows how large it is, how much room this device reports, and how the download is going.

Opening this page does not download a region. Looking at a map once, or leaving the page open, is not a download either. A region is installed only after every part has arrived and been checked against its fingerprint.

The badge Ready offline appears when the pack is installed and the page itself is being kept by the browser. If the badge is missing, the page tells you what is still needed.

2. Make the page open without the Ark

Browsers only let a page work offline when it is served over a connection the device trusts. The Ark makes its own certificate because it has no public name and may have no internet. Trusting it once, on that device, is what turns Downloaded into Ready offline.

  1. On the Ark’s ordinary page, tap Download the Ark’s certificate. (The file is also offered at http://ark.local/certificate.crt.)
  2. Install it as a trusted certificate on the device — the steps for each kind of device are below.
  3. Open https://ark.local:8446/maps/ in the browser. The address must begin with https and end with :8446; the ordinary http://ark.local cannot keep a page offline.
  4. Add the page to the home screen, or bookmark it. That is the way back in when the Ark is gone.

iPhone or iPad

Download the certificate, then open Settings → Profile Downloaded → Install. Then open Settings → General → About → Certificate Trust Settings and switch on full trust for “Project ARK”. Open the Maps address in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Android

Download the certificate, then open Settings → Security → Encryption & credentials → Install a certificate → CA certificate. Choose the downloaded file. Open the Maps address in Chrome, open the menu, then Add to Home screen.

Laptop

Import the certificate into the operating system’s trusted certificates, open the Maps address, and bookmark it. On macOS, Keychain Access can trust a certificate for this device only; on Windows, use “Trusted Root Certification Authorities”.

If you cannot trust the certificate, Maps still works while the Ark is reachable — it just cannot promise to reopen with the Ark gone. Never install a certificate you have not compared with the fingerprint shown by the Ark’s owner.

3. Use the map away from the Ark

Open the Maps icon or bookmark. The page, the region, the search index and the routing graph are all read from the device. Pan and zoom anywhere in the downloaded region — including places you never looked at before — search for a name, choose a start and a destination, and tap Show route.

The route is calculated on the device. You can print a route sheet, download one as a file, or download the route as GPX for another program. A saved route sheet is a paper fallback; it is not a substitute for calculating a new route offline, which Maps does itself.

Outside the downloaded region the map says so instead of showing a blank square. The dashed line marks the edge of what was downloaded.

What the directions can and cannot know

  • One-way streets, access rules and the turn restrictions carried in the map data are respected. Restrictions that use a whole road as their “via” part, or that depend on a time of day, are not carried.
  • Times use the speed of each kind of road, not live traffic, closures, weather or roadworks.
  • Street addresses are usually incomplete or absent. Search finds named places, settlements and points of interest; you can also type coordinates or tap a point on the map.
  • A mapped water source is not a claim that the water is drinkable. A mapped clinic, shop or shelter is not a claim that it is open, stocked or staffed. Maps is a map, not a promise about the world.
  • On foot, a route ignores car one-way rules, but it can only use roads and paths that are in the pack.

If something is missing or damaged

Open Packs on this device and use Check this copy. It reads every part and compares it with the fingerprint recorded when the pack was built. If a part is missing or does not match, remove the copy and download it again while the Ark is reachable. A half-finished download can be continued; the pieces already received are kept.

Browsers may remove stored data when the device runs out of room. Maps asks the browser to keep it and says whether that request was granted. Keeping the Ark reachable, or keeping a second device with the same pack, is the practical backup.

Privacy

Searches, saved places, the current location (if you choose to use it), the last view and route history stay on this device. Maps does not send them to the Ark. The map is drawn from a pack; there is no online map service, no account and no tracking.

You can remove saved places and route history from this device at any time from the map page, and remove a pack from Packs on this device. Removing a pack here does not remove the Ark’s copy or a copy on another phone.
